## Commentary

The week ran dearer than its label suggests. Against the previous 13 weeks the mean ranks 4th of 14, at 18.42 c/kWh. The tails say more: 280 of 336 half-hours cleared above €150, against a typical 232, and 102 cleared above €200, against a typical 72.

Wind moved the days around. Wednesday 30 Sep was the cheapest at 14.25 c/kWh and Saturday 3 Oct the dearest at 21.58. But wind averaged 1743.1 MW against a 13-week median of 865.6 MW, and the week still sat near the top of the range. Wind alone didn't keep it cheap.

The 13 weeks are mostly summer, so "normal" here means normal for a baseline that has been rising. This is the spot market. Fixed contract prices are set separately, and nothing in this data speaks to them.
