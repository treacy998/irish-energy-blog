## Commentary

€414.90 at 18:00. The highest price on the blog, just edging out June 24th's €414.20.
Wind averaged 10.5% and faded to 3.8% at 17:30, right as the evening demand arrived. The price jumped €127 in one half-hour (€269.72 at 16:30 → €396.36 at 17:00) and stayed above €400 until 19:30.

That's a scarcity event, not a normal peak. Low wind, high demand (4,162 MW), and the market reaching deep into the most expensive units to cover the evening.
The rest of the day was ordinary: €180 overnight, €260 morning, €186 midday.

Three hours decided the whole day. A €231.65 cheap/dear spread, second only to last Thursday's €242.
