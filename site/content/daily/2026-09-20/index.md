## Commentary

From €52 to €240 in one day. Wind went from 35% overnight to 4% by 09:30, and the price went to €359.68, on a Sunday morning.
The whole 08:00-10:30 block sat between €350 and €360. Lowest demand of the fortnight (3,641 MW) and still one of the dearest mornings we've seen.

The afternoon was choppy, bouncing between €211 and €298 with wind stuck under 10%. The overnight floor (€140) was the only cheap window, which is why the peak/off-peak spread hit €97.39, the widest of the run.

Proof that weekend demand is no protection. With no wind, Sunday costs more than a weekday.
