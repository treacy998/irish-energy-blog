## Commentary

Steady wind all day (29.7-54.7%) and the price shape ignored it. The cheapest block of the day, €157-160 from 11:30 to 15:30, came when wind was at its lowest (~30%).
The overnight hours had 50%+ wind and still sat at €191-215.

When wind is flat, demand draws the shape: morning peak €248 at 07:30, a flat midday floor, evening peak €253.90 at 18:30. A textbook weekday.

The peak/off-peak spread went slightly negative (-€1.64) because overnight was about as dear as the working day. Steady wind lowers the level, but doesn't flatten the shape.
