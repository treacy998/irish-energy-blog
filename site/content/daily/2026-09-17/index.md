## Commentary

This is what cannibalisation looks like. Wind held 44-55% all day and the midday bottom fell out: €22.16 at 12:30, and sub-€30 from 11:30 through 14:30.
Big wind + midday demand lull → the clearing price falls through the gas units and down into the renewable end of the merit order.

Overnight had the same wind but only got to €153. The evening came back hard once wind eased to 39%, peaking at €280 at 21:00.
Mean of €147.56, but a cheap/dear spread of €242. Charge at €24, discharge at €266.

Best battery day of the fortnight by a distance. Give us another one Friday.
