## Commentary

€14.73 at 14:30. Sunday demand was even lower than Saturday (3,686 MW), and with wind around 40% the midday floor almost disappeared: under €45 from 13:00 to 15:30.

Then the wind left. It dropped from 51% at 16:00 to 19% by 21:00, and the price went from €103 to €290 in the same window. The 21:00 spike is the day's peak, late for a peak and well above the €245-€255 either side of it.
The morning was a smaller version of the same story: wind down to 24%, price up to €240.71 at 10:00.

A €240.40 cheap/dear spread, second only to the €242 on the 17th. Wind is still low heading into Monday. That's not a good sign.
