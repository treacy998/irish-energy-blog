## Commentary

The weekend caught it, sort of. Wind sat above 70% for most of the night and prices only drifted from €196 to €146. Overnight demand is too low for wind to do much damage to price.

The real trough came mid-morning: €80.30 at 10:00, with wind already falling back to 34%. Saturday demand, not peak wind, is what opened that floor.
Then the wind collapsed through the afternoon (8% by 21:00) and the evening turned into a scarcity run, topping out at €323.88 at 22:00.

Std dev of €69.64 and a €213 cheap/dear spread make this the most volatile day in a while. Charge at €80 before lunch, discharge at €294 after dark. If you're flexible, this was the day.
