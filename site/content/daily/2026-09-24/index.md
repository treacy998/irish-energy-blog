## Commentary

Wind came back like we hoped. 44.9% on average and the price came down with it: a mean of €175.61, down €35 on Wednesday.

The overnight is where it shows most. Wind sat at 53-58% from 01:30 to 06:00 and the price drifted down to €155. Midday was the floor, €122-€126 from 13:00 to 15:30 with wind back around 50%.
The evening still bit though. Wind faded from 49% at 16:00 to 26% by 21:00 and the price climbed to €227.06 at 19:30.

Better, not cheap. Only 10 of 48 periods below €150.
