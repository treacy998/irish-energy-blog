## Commentary

A Sunday with every single period above €200. That's not a typo.
Wind averaged 5.7% and spent the morning below 1%, so gas set the price all day. The floor was €201 at 05:00, on the quietest demand of the fortnight so far (3,748 MW).

Flat and high: std dev just €25.25, a morning bump to €262.65 at 10:00 and an evening peak of €291.23 at 20:00. No trough, no relief, nothing cheap anywhere.

Low demand can't save you when there's no wind. Monday won't be kinder unless it blows.
